# Contributing

Suggest a case with an issue ([template](../../issues/new/choose)). We add it when:

- it describes a real product problem at a named company and the method used to solve it;
- the solution can transfer to other companies;
- it is public (engineering blog, conference talk, paper).

Vendor marketing and panel discussions are left out. The lists are generated from [effectsize.io](https://effectsize.io),
so accepted suggestions appear here and on the site after the next update.
